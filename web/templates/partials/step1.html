What's your priority?

Select your optimization goal. In-S8 will intelligently test the most relevant architectures for your use case.

🎯 How it works

Throughput Priority: Benchmarks both Aggregated and Expert Parallelism (EP) architectures, then recommends the configuration that maximizes requests/second and tokens/second for your specific ISL/OSL workload.

Response Time Priority: Benchmarks both Aggregated and Prefill/Decode (PD) disaggregation architectures, then recommends the configuration that minimizes Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) for your specific ISL/OSL workload.

Balanced: Evaluates all three architectures (Aggregated, EP, and PD) to identify the optimal configuration that provides acceptable performance across both TTFT and throughput metrics.

Throughput Priority

Optimizes for maximum requests/second and tokens/second. Compares traditional Aggregated inference against Expert Parallelism (EP) to identify which architecture delivers superior throughput for your workload.

Benchmarks: Aggregated vs Expert Parallelism

Response Time Priority

Optimizes for minimum Time-to-First-Token (TTFT). Compares traditional Aggregated inference against Prefill/Decode (PD) disaggregation to determine which architecture provides faster initial response times.

Benchmarks: Aggregated vs Prefill/Decode

Balanced Performance

Evaluates all three architectures to find the optimal configuration that balances both TTFT and throughput. Ideal when you need good performance across multiple metrics without extreme optimization in one direction.

Benchmarks: Aggregated, EP, and PD
