Cluster Capacity Analysis

Math - No Tests

Calculates GPU cost per request from TPSG measurements:

• Costprefill = ISL ÷ TPSGprefill (GPU-seconds)
• Costdecode = OSL ÷ TPSGdecode (GPU-seconds)

Pure math - instant results

5

Sustainable Throughput & Enumerate Splits

Math - No Tests

Derives cluster capacity and ideal P/D ratio:

• Ideal prefill % = Costprefill ÷ (Costprefill + Costdecode)
• Sustainable QPS = Total GPUs ÷ Total Cost ÷ 1.3 headroom
• Detects if concurrency implies load beyond sustainable capacity

TP pair selection (cross-product):

• Takes the top-2 prefill TPs from Step 3 and top-2 decode TPs from Step 2
• Builds a cross-product of all combinations (up to 4 pairs)
• Supports asymmetric TP — prefill and decode can use different TP values (e.g., PTP=8, DTP=1)
• Example: if top prefill = [TP4, TP8] and top decode = [TP8, TP4], tests pairs: (4,8), (4,4), (8,8), (8,4)

Split enumeration and trimming:

• Generates all valid P/D splits for each TP pair, respecting TP granularity
• If total splits exceed the budget, trims with fairness: at least one split per TP pair is guaranteed (closest to ideal ratio), then remaining slots filled by proximity to ideal
• This means splits far from the ideal ratio (e.g., 75%+ prefill) may be excluded when budget is tight

Pure math - instant results

Exhaustive Search

Latency-Bounded Throughput Maximization

Conditional

Triggers when: "Maximize Throughput under Latency SLA" is enabled in Test Configuration

What runs: Binary search over concurrency levels for both the best PD and best Aggregated configurations independently

Why: Finds the maximum throughput your cluster can deliver while keeping TTFT under your latency target at the specified percentile

Search algorithm:

• Phase 1 — Ramp-up: Starts at the calibrated sustainable QPS from Step 4. If TTFT is well below the target, scales concurrency proportionally; otherwise doubles it each step. Continues until TTFT exceeds the SLA, establishing an upper bound
• Phase 2 — Bisection: Binary search between the highest passing concurrency (low) and the lowest failing concurrency (high). Each step tests the midpoint and narrows the bracket
• Stopping condition: Converges when the highest passing and lowest failing concurrency are close enough that further testing would not meaningfully improve throughput. Example: low=200 (passes), high=208 (fails) → the gap is negligible, search stops at c=200
• Decision metric: Only TTFT at the user-selected percentile (e.g., P99) is used for pass/fail decisions. All other percentiles (P50, P90, P95) are recorded for analysis

Runs for both: Best PD config and best Aggregated config — picks the winner by highest throughput that meets the SLA

Result: Optimal concurrency per architecture, with full percentile data at every tested concurrency level (enables cost-of-latency analysis)

Calibrated Load Validation

Conditional

Triggers when: The user's concurrency implies load beyond sustainable cluster capacity AND "Scale to Sustainable Load" is disabled

What runs: Re-tests the best PD and Aggregated configs at a sustainable concurrency level

Why: Steps 7-8 ran under overload, inflating latency. This step shows realistic performance at a load the cluster can actually handle

Result: Separate comparison showing calibrated vs overloaded metrics, with TTFT improvement percentage

11

EPP Tuning

Conditional

Triggers when: "Benchmark EPP Strategies" is enabled in Step 5 (EPP Config)

What runs: Takes the best deployment and tests 3 EPP scoring weight combinations, swapping only the gateway configmap (~10s per swap, no pod redeployment)

Why: The EPP scoring weights (prefix-cache vs KV-utilization vs queue depth) significantly affect how requests are routed to pods. The optimal balance depends on your workload's cache hit rate and sequence lengths

Weight combos tested:

  • Cache-heavy (5:1:1) — maximizes prefix cache reuse
  • Queue-heavy (1:1:5) — prevents pod hotspotting
  • KV-heavy or Equal — based on ISL/OSL ratio

Result: Identifies the scoring weights that give the best TTFT for your specific workload
